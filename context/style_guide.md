# Style guide (human-readable; the checks in validate.py enforce the mechanical parts)
- Audience: residents and visitors in Innlandet, incl. people who do not read Norwegian well.
- Tone: calm, factual, no alarmism. Level colours exactly as the official Varsom scale.
- Facts allowed: those in the source item only. You write a short summary; the pipeline appends the complete official Norwegian text unaltered.
- Do not write links. The pipeline adds the official source link and attribution.
- The digest is advisory only; for emergencies call 110/112 (added by the pipeline).
